# Code of Conduct

## Make room for people

I want PP-MD to be a place where people feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, and admitting they don't know something yet. Whether this is your first Issue or your fiftieth Pull Request, you're welcome here.

Different experiences make this project better. Treat people with respect regardless of disability, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, appearance, or level of experience.

- Be kind, patient, and constructive. Challenge an idea without attacking the person.
- Respect people's names, pronouns, boundaries, and privacy.
- Keep language clear and make room for different communication and access needs.
- Listen when someone explains the impact of your words or actions. If you get it wrong, acknowledge it and change course.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention or content, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information aren't acceptable. Neither is retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## If something goes wrong

This applies to Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other spaces connected with this project, including when representing it elsewhere.

For a conduct concern, contact me, Mike Hartley, privately through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mikebhartley/). Share only what you're comfortable sharing. Please don't open a public Issue containing personal details or allegations about someone else.

I'll review concerns fairly and handle information as privately as possible, sharing it only where needed to address the situation. Depending on the behaviour and its impact, I may request a change, issue a warning, remove content, or restrict or end someone's participation. Serious or repeated harm can mean an immediate ban.

We don't have to agree on everything. We do need to treat each other as people.
